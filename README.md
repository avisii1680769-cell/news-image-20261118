A 1024x1024 newspaper-style front page summarising the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026, drawn with PIL. Six stories: US strikes Iran, Trump/Xi/Putin meet, Ukraine hits Crimea bridge, US loses measles-free status, tech outage hits US government, and Cyclone Akvan. `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
